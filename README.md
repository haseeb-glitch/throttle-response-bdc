# AI BDC Platform (SMS-First)

## Overview

AI BDC Platform is an intelligent Business Development Center (BDC) solution designed to automate lead engagement for dealerships through natural, human-like SMS conversations. The platform helps businesses respond to new leads, nurture customer relationships, qualify prospects, and assist sales teams in converting conversations into appointments.

The system is designed with a strong focus on simplicity, automation, and conversion performance while allowing seamless human intervention whenever needed.

## Features

- AI-powered SMS conversations
- Natural, human-like customer engagement
- Real-time lead response automation
- Lead management dashboard
- Appointment tracking
- Buying probability scoring
- Manual agent takeover
- Automated follow-up workflows
- Operating hours automation
- Lead prioritization
- Secondary email support
- Scalable architecture for future integrations
